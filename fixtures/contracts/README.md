# Contract fixtures

Files in `valid/` are repository-owned, text-only examples for schema and
cross-document conformance. The contract manifest marks them
`schema-conformance-only`.

They do not describe an executed build. Hashes are fictional syntax tokens,
and executor/schema/QA identifiers containing `fixture-placeholder` are
deliberately unresolved. These examples are not capability conformance
fixtures, do not accept a presentation input, and do not support a renderer
claim. `policy/support-matrix.json` remains authoritative.
